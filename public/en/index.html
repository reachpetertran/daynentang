About the English pages

The site is written in Vietnamese for Vietnamese families. We are translating it step by step. The first English course week is Family 2–8: Week 1. Pages marked (Vietnamese) are not translated yet; your browser’s Translate option can help with those.

Every course makes two promises

Help now

A real result you can see within 1–2 weeks.

Build for the long term

One way of thinking and one skill you can use for years.

Four pillars

Skills

Looking after yourself, planning, putting first things first, managing money and housework.

Character

Honesty, gratitude, kindness, responsibility, a good work ethic.

Mind

Naming feelings, short breathing, staying calm when angry, knowing when to ask for help.

Body

Sleep, movement, family meals, safety at home.

How we work

Every lesson lists its sources and a label for how certain each claim is. We borrow only ideas from copyrighted programmes and rewrite everything. Lessons are tried first in the author’s own family. Read our principles.
