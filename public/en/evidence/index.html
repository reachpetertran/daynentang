Evidence and sources

Every important claim on the site carries one of four labels.

  • [Verified] – we have read the source; it is named next to the claim.
  • [Inference] – drawn from a source, but the source does not say it directly.
  • [Expert judgement] – the author’s view; no study of its own yet.
  • [Needs checking] – no reliable source found yet.
