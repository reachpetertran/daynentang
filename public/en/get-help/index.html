Get help now

If you or someone else is in danger, call now. This website does not replace a doctor, a psychologist or the authorities.

In Vietnam

NumberWhen to callHours
115Medical emergency24/7
111National child protection hotline: a child is abused, hurt, abandoned, or needs advice24/7, free
096 306 1414Ngày Mai hotline: people with depression or thoughts of self-harm, and their families (in Vietnamese)13:00–20:30, Wednesday to Sunday
117National domestic violence hotline: advice, support, referral to services (running since September 2026)24/7
112Search and rescue24/7
113 / 114Police / Fire24/7

Checked on 28 October 2026 from tongdai111.vn and duongdaynongngaymai.vn. We re-check every quarter. If a number does not answer, call 115 or go to the nearest health facility. Most lines answer in Vietnamese.

Outside Vietnam

  • Call your local emergency number straight away if someone is in danger.
  • Find A Helpline lists free, confidential crisis lines in many countries (run by ThroughLine).

While you wait

  • Stay with the person in danger, if you are safe.
  • Move dangerous things away: knives, medicines, cords.
  • Speak slowly and listen more. You don’t need the right words; just be there.
