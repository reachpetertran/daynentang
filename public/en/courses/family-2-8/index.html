Family 2–8: The Foundation Hand

Parents, even one parent alone, build three foundations through 30 minutes of purposeful play: self-reliant habits, a curious, testing mind, and kind values. And there is less shouting at home.

ForParents, grandparents and carers of children aged 2–8
Length12 weeks × 3 sessions × 30 minutes. About 30 minutes of preparation per week
You needThings already at home, paper, chunky crayons. Children need no screens
StatusAll 12 weeks written in Vietnamese. Week 1 is translated and was tried in one family; weeks 2–12 are not yet tried

Help now (1–2 weeks)

  • A fun play time with your child every day.
  • Your child does at least one small job alone.
  • You have ready-made phrases to use instead of shouting.

Build for the long term

  • Executive function: attention, working memory, self-control.
  • The habit of asking, guessing and checking.
  • Honesty, gratitude, kindness, responsibility.

The Foundation Hand

Five fingers are five habits. Children look at their own hand and remember.

The 12 weeks

Three sessions each week: T Habits, K Hands-on science (Look → Guess → Try → Tell), G Values and mindfulness. Week 1 is in English. For weeks 2–12, open the Vietnamese course page; your browser can translate it.

“Older child” = about 7 years old; “toddler” = about 2 years old. Adjust to your own children’s ages.

One 30-minute session

  1. Connect (0–3′): say hello, sit close. The toddler sits on your lap.
  2. Breathe for 1 minute (3–5′): “smell the flower – blow out the candle”.
  3. Main activity (5–20′): two levels, for the older child and the toddler.
  4. Child retells (20–25′): the toddler points, names or copies.
  5. Take it into daily life (25–30′): pick one thing to do this week, add a sticker.

A 2-year-old joins for 10–15 minutes, then plays alongside. Don’t make them sit for the full 30 minutes.

Track progress

Score the observation chart (Vietnamese) at weeks 0, 6 and 12: 0 = not yet, 1 = with a reminder, 2 = on their own. Also count, over 3 days, how often you give specific praise and how often you shout. This is self-tracking, not a test.

Limits, honestly

[Inference] Most of these programmes were tested in groups with a facilitator. Self-study at home has not been shown to work as well. This course is being tried in one family, with no comparison group. We make no promises about grades or IQ.

Safety for every session

  • Under 3s: keep small objects out of reach. Home tip: anything that fits through a toilet-paper tube is too small.
  • No button batteries, small magnets, plastic bags or sharp knives and scissors near the toddler.
  • Water play: an adult always within arm’s reach; empty the water right after.
